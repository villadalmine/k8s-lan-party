Party actions

Your consent is stored in your namespace. A party opens after both players agree and the host reconciles it.

The house match

The host may run a house match: its own Doom game, open to every player. It is broadcast on the public page (/live): anyone can watch it, with you in it, while you play. Joining is your consent to that; leaving ends it. Once the party is active, the game is in Games I can join: press Play.

Watching or playing a game

Build the pinned Doom image with poc/doom/build.sh, then import its tarball into k3s on this node with sudo k3s ctr images import <tarball>. Enter the printed local tag (localhost/emesh-doom:12.3.0- plus the 12-character build hash). The spectator runs in your namespace and uses an authenticated frame proxy. Play starts your own game client the same way, on your machine: the picture comes to this tab and your keys go back to it, never through the host.
